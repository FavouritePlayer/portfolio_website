Face Morpher: From-Scratch Warping

Computer Vision
NumPydlibscipyOpenCV

Face averaging and morphing implemented from first principles: dlib landmark correspondence, scipy Delaunay triangulation, a per-triangle affine solve (three-point correspondence to a 2×3 matrix), and inverse warping with bilinear interpolation to blend faces onto a midway shape. All core geometry is hand-written in NumPy; OpenCV is used only for I/O and polygon masking.
